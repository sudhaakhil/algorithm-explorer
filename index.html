    Methodology and limitations

    All four algorithms receive identical target and denominations. Brute Force here uses memoization to avoid repeated subproblems, while Backtracking uses branch pruning. Both are recursive approaches. The app limits amounts to keep computation responsive. Coin supply is unlimited; only positive integers are supported. Greedy is not always optimal.
